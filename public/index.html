🚨 How to Restore the App if Hidden

If you use the 🕵️ Hide App Icon command, the app will disappear from the phone. To bring it back:

  • Method 1 (Secret Code): Open the phone's dialer and type *#*#240113#*#*. The app will instantly open.
  • Method 2 (USB/ADB): Connect to a PC and run: adb shell pm unhide com.lastbreath.antitheft.pro

🔒 What is the DRM ID?

The Widevine DRM ID is a hardware identifier burned into the phone's motherboard by the manufacturer. It survives factory resets and USB flashing.

  • When you mark a device as "Stolen", the server traps its DRM ID.
  • If a thief flashes the phone and installs the app again, the server detects the same DRM ID and emails you a warning with the thief's new IMEI!

⚠️ Important Warning

Commands like Change PIN, Factory Reset, and Wipe Data are permanent. Test them on a spare phone before using them on your primary device.
